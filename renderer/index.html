Paper and toner status come from what each printer driver reports to Windows. Many printers only report paper problems (low, out, jam) and not an exact level; "Not reported" means the driver gave no information.

Print Agent

Prints paid orders automatically and reports real printer health (offline, jam, out of paper, low ink) to your dashboard.
